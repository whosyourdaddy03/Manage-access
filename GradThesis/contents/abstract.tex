\begin{ChineseAbstract}

低空无人机距离较远时，在普通相机画面中往往只占少量像素，易受背景干扰；遇到遮挡或漏检时，航迹还容易中断。针对这些问题，本文使用单目可见光相机，设计并实现了无人机识别与定位系统，主要完成以下三项工作。

（1）设计并实现了无人机监视与仿真验证系统。搭建包含建筑、树木和预设航迹的三维仿真场景，支持仿真画面、视频文件和摄像头输入。检测、跟踪与定位在后台运行，界面实时显示目标框、地图航迹与距离告警，支持数据保存和历史回放，并结合仿真真值计算定位误差，完整处理速度达每秒32帧。

（2）设计了无人机三态跟踪与单目定位方法。将跟踪分为搜索、锁定和短时预测三种状态：确认检测可靠后建立锁定并限制相邻帧关联范围；遇到遮挡或漏检时按此前运动状态短时预测位置，目标重现后修正航迹。结合目标在画面中的位置、大小和相机参数计算方向与距离，并通过卡尔曼滤波减小结果波动。测试中，遮挡场景航迹保持率达88.9\%--94.5\%，仿真稳态测距、方位角和1\,s预测的中位误差分别为0.13\,m、0.05$^{\circ}$和0.26\,m。

（3）提出了面向小无人机的检测改进与仿真微调方法。在识别网络中增加高分辨率检测层保留目标细节，结合注意力模块突出目标区域；训练数据按拍摄时间划分并设置100帧间隔。针对仿真画面中的漏检问题，按目标距离与成像大小自动采集标注450帧样本进行微调。公开数据集上改进模型的mAP50由81.3\%提高到83.5\%，小目标平均精度由6.1\%提高到9.8\%，处理速度为每秒98.4帧；微调后在2818帧仿真记录上检出率达100\%。

上述工作形成了从仿真构建、模型适配到连续跟踪定位与可视化验证的完整系统。

\ChineseKeywords{反无人机；小目标检测；特征增强；仿真域微调；三态跟踪；单目定位}

\end{ChineseAbstract}

\begin{EnglishAbstract}

At long ranges, low-altitude unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) occupy only a few pixels in camera images and are prone to background interference, while occlusions or missed detections easily interrupt tracks. To address this, this thesis implements a monocular visible-light UAV recognition and localization system in three parts.

First, a UAV monitoring and simulation validation system is implemented. A 3D simulation scene with buildings, trees, and preset trajectories supports simulation streams, video files, and camera inputs. Detection, tracking, and localization run in the background while the interface provides bounding boxes, map tracks, distance alerts, data storage, replay, and ground-truth error evaluation at 32\,fps.

Second, a three-state UAV tracking and monocular localization method is designed. Tracking switches among search, lock, and short-term prediction states: reliable detections initiate locking with constrained inter-frame association, while motion prediction maintains tracks during brief occlusions until reappearance. Direction and distance are estimated from image position, size, and camera parameters with Kalman filtering, achieving 88.9\%--94.5\% track retention under occlusion and simulation median errors of 0.13\,m in steady-state ranging, 0.05$^{\circ}$ in azimuth, and 0.26\,m in 1\,s prediction.

Third, an improved small-UAV detection and simulation fine-tuning method is developed. A high-resolution detection layer and an attention module are introduced to preserve fine details and highlight targets, with chronological data splitting and a 100-frame gap. Collecting and auto-annotating 450 simulation frames across distances for fine-tuning raises public-dataset mAP50 from 81.3\% to 83.5\% and small-object AP from 6.1\% to 9.8\% at 98.4\,fps, achieving a 100\% detection rate across 2818 simulation frames.

Overall, this work forms a complete system from simulation construction and model adaptation to continuous tracking, localization, and visual validation.

\EnglishKeywords{counter-UAV; small object detection; feature enhancement; simulation-domain fine-tuning; three-state tracking; monocular localization}

\end{EnglishAbstract}
