\begin{ChineseAbstract}

\textcolor{black}{低空无人机距离较远时，在普通相机画面中往往只占少量像素，容易受到背景干扰；被遮挡后，检测还容易中断。针对这些问题，本文使用一台可见光相机，设计并实现了无人机识别与定位系统，}主要完成以下三项工作。

（1）设计并实现了一套无人机监视与仿真验证系统。系统构建了包含障碍建筑和飞行轨迹的三维仿真场景，能够实时获取无人机画面。系统在画面中用目标框标出识别到的无人机，在地图上显示飞行航迹，并提供状态显示、按距离分级告警、数据保存和历史回放等功能。检测、跟踪和定位等计算任务在后台运行，避免影响界面操作。同时，系统保存仿真中的目标真实位置和算法估计位置，通过比较两者计算定位误差，检验系统的实际效果。

（2）设计了一种用于连续跟踪无人机，并对其进行定位的方法；将跟踪的状态变化分为三种，分别是：搜索，锁定和预测。系统确认当前检测对象可靠后进行锁定，并通过对比画面中出现的目标位置和偏移量，避免检测对象锁定错误。当检测对象被树或森林遮挡或短暂丢失检测目标时，系统会根据对象之前的运动状态进行位置预测；当目标重新出现后，用实际检测结果修正运动轨迹。系统结合目标在画面中的位置，大小及相机参数估计无人机的方向与距离，并通过滤波减少结果波动。最后，通过遮挡恢复、距离估计和轨迹预测实验方法效果。

（3）提出了一种面向小无人机的检测改进方法。\textcolor{black}{本文在图像识别网络中增加了能够保留更多图像细节的检测分支，即增加一条根据图像特征输出目标框的计算路径，并让网络更加关注目标区域，}从而提高小目标与背景的区分能力。训练数据按拍摄时间划分，并在训练集和验证集之间留出一段画面，避免相邻图像过于相似而使验证结果偏高。针对模型用于仿真画面后出现的漏检问题，本文根据目标距离及其在画面中的大小分析原因，自动采集并标注仿真样本，再对模型进行较小幅度的调整，使其更好地适应仿真场景。

\end{ChineseAbstract}

\begin{EnglishAbstract}

When flying at long ranges, low-altitude unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) often occupy only a small number of pixels in images captured by a conventional camera and are susceptible to background interference; occlusions can further interrupt detection. To address these problems, this thesis designs and implements a UAV recognition and localization system using a single visible-light camera. The main work comprises the following three aspects.

First, a UAV monitoring and simulation validation system is designed and implemented. A three-dimensional simulation scene containing obstacle buildings and flight trajectories is constructed to acquire UAV imagery in real time. The system marks recognized UAVs with bounding boxes, displays flight tracks on a map, and provides status display, distance-based graded alerts, data storage, and historical replay. Detection, tracking, and localization are performed in the background to maintain responsive interface operation. The system also records the ground-truth and estimated target positions in simulation and calculates localization errors through their comparison, thereby supporting the evaluation of system performance.

Second, a three-state method for continuous UAV tracking and localization is designed, dividing the tracking process into search, lock, and short-term prediction. Tracking begins after the detections are confirmed to be reliable, and target positions and distances in consecutive frames are compared to prevent incorrect target association. When a target is occluded or temporarily missed, its position is predicted for a short period according to its previous motion state. Once the target reappears, the actual detections are used to correct the track. The target direction and distance are then estimated from its position and size in the image together with the camera parameters, and filtering is applied to reduce fluctuations in the estimates. Experiments on occlusion recovery, distance estimation, and trajectory prediction are conducted to evaluate the method.

Third, an improved detection method for small UAVs is developed. A detection branch that preserves more image details is added to the recognition network, providing an additional computational path that generates bounding boxes from image features and directing greater network attention toward target regions. These improvements enhance the discrimination between small targets and the background. Training data are divided chronologically, with a frame interval between the training and validation sets to prevent overly similar adjacent images from inflating the validation results. To address missed detections after the model is transferred to simulation imagery, the causes are analysed according to target distance and projected size. Simulation samples are then automatically collected and annotated, and the model is moderately fine-tuned to improve its adaptation to the simulation scenario.

\EnglishKeywords{counter-UAV; small object detection; feature enhancement; simulation-domain fine-tuning; three-state tracking; monocular localization}

\end{EnglishAbstract}
